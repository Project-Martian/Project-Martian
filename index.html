          The contribution guide
Evidence first.
People review it.

          
  1. Check the archive
    Search Timeline first. Add new evidence to an existing record instead of reporting the same event twice.
    Browse the timeline →
  2. Write one sourced report
    Separate what happened from what the source claims. Keep occurrence and disclosure dates distinct. Unknown is a useful answer.
  3. Follow your GitHub issue
    Your issue is the review thread. The team checks the sources, resolves duplicates and prepares the structured incident.
  4. Approval before publication
    Once verified, the team approves the final record and marks the issue Done. The publication worker adds that record to the database and keeps the issue linked.

          

          
Useful reports include
Public incidents, documented research demonstrations, corrections and source follow-ups. Describe evidence and effects; do not include attack instructions for live systems.
