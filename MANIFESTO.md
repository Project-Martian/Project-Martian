# Publication principles

Project Martian is a public record of reported AI-agent safety and security incidents. Link claims to their sources, write plainly, retain uncertainty, and correct the record when stronger evidence appears.

Incident reports, research evaluations and independently verified outcomes carry different evidence. A source link is not independent reproduction, and reported financial impact must retain its attribution. The bundled records are manually curated publication content.

Keep the publication independent: use only its own runtime and public evidence, with no external product or customer-data dependency. Public contributions belong in this repository; private credentials and customer records do not.
